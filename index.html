Hi, I'm Alivia!

I'm a young maker who loves sewing, crafting, and creating things that are as unique as the people who wear and use them. Every item in my shop is homemade by me with fabrics I pick out because they make me smile. Anything from whimsical celestial prints to cozy fall patterns.

Whether you're looking for a one-of-a-kind cape, a cute bookmark for your favorite novel, or a custom tote bag, I'd love to make something special for you!

How It Works

A few straightforward answers about ordering from my shop.

How do I order?

Browse the shop, open a product, and add ready-to-ship listings or a custom request to your basket. Then send the order form on the shop page. I'll reply by email or Instagram to confirm the details. A few pieces may offer a direct “Buy now” checkout link; everything else goes through the request form.

What does “ready to ship” vs. “made to order” mean?

Ready to ship means the piece is already sewn and waiting; what you see is what arrives. Made to order means I make it after you ask, so we can pick the fabric and details together first.

Can I request a custom fabric or size?

Yes! Use a product page or the order form and describe what you have in mind. The custom page shows which pieces can be made to order. I'll confirm fabric availability when I reply.
